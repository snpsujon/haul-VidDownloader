Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

What Haul collects

Nothing. Haul has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads. We never receive the links you save or the files you download.

What Haul connects to

  • The sites you paste or share links from, to read and download the media you asked for.
  • GitHub, to update the yt-dlp download engine (at most once a day, on Wi-Fi, if you leave that on).
  • This website, to check for new versions of the app (once a day, if you leave that on). That request carries no personal data; our host may log the IP address as any web server does.

What stays on your phone

Downloaded files, your Library, your settings and the error logs you can copy from the Queue. Uninstalling Haul removes everything except the files you saved to Download/Haul or another folder you chose.

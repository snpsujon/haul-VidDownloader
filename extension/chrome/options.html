One-tap send to phone

Pair your phone once. After that, Send to phone goes straight to Haul on it, with no QR code to scan. Links pass through the Haul website and are deleted once your phone picks them up.

Scan this with your phone camera and confirm in Haul. Waiting for your phone… The code works for 10 minutes.

      Download on this PC

      Checking…

      To save videos on this computer, Haul needs its small helper app. You only do this once.

      1. Download haul-helper.exe for Windows.
      2. Double-click it. It copies itself to your user folder, downloads yt-dlp and FFmpeg (about 150 MB), and connects to Chrome, Edge and Brave. No admin rights needed.
      3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. That happens because the helper isn't code-signed yet.
      4. When it says it's done, come back here and click Check again.

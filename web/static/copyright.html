Copyright and DMCA

Last updated October 27, 2026

Haul respects copyright. Haul doesn't host, store or index any media. Files are downloaded from the original site at the request of the user, and files made on our server are deleted shortly after.

If you believe Haul is being used to infringe your copyright, or something on this site (like a file offered for download) infringes it, send a notice through the contact page with:

  1. Your name and how to contact you.
  2. The work you own, and the exact links involved.
  3. A statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorized by you, your agent or the law.
  4. A statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or allowed to act for the owner.
  5. Your physical or electronic signature.

We'll look at valid notices promptly and may block the links involved from being downloaded through this server.

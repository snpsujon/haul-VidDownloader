About Haul

Last updated October 27, 2026

Haul saves video and audio from YouTube and more than 1,000 other sites. Paste a link, pick the quality, audio language, subtitles or a clip, and download. It's free, with no account and no ads.

Ways to use it

  • This website, on any device.
  • The browser extension with the PC helper, which downloads straight to your computer in full quality.
  • The Android app, which downloads on your phone and can get links sent from your PC.

Get them from the Get Haul section on the home page.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

Where do downloads happen?

With the extension and PC helper installed, on your own computer. Otherwise on our server, which deletes each file a few minutes after you download it.

Why did a download fail?

Sites change often and sometimes block downloads for a while. Try again later, or try a lower quality. Private, members-only and some age-restricted videos can't be downloaded.

Can I download a whole playlist?

Yes. Paste the playlist link, pick the videos, and download them as one zip. You can also paste several links at once.

Is it legal?

It depends on what you download and where you live. Only save content you own or have permission to save. See the terms.

What does it do with my data?

Very little. See the privacy policy.
